## Description (paste into the "description" field)

```
Iterra is a free, local-first study workspace that replaces the eight
apps students duct-tape together: a course planner, spaced-repetition
flashcards, linked notes, a pomodoro timer, a math workbench, an
attendance register, bubble-sheet grading, and a study-material
organizer — all in one place.

No account. No subscription. No cloud lock-in. Your notes, cards,
schedules, and files live in your browser, work offline, and are
exportable. The tools share a live picture of your courses, so your
planner knows when reviews are due and your notes surface in course
dossiers automatically.

Built for students who are tired of paying for five subscriptions
that don't talk to each other.
```

## First comment (post immediately after launch)

```
Hey Product Hunt — I'm [Maker Name], the person who built Iterra.

I got tired of the student-tool landscape: Notion for notes, Quizlet
for flashcards, a pomodoro app for focus, a separate planner, and a
separate grade tracker — eight subscriptions, eight logins, zero
integration, and all my data scattered across servers I don't control.

Iterra is my answer: one workspace where every tool knows about every
other tool. When a review is due, it shows up on your Today view.
When you file a handout in Margin, your notes can cite it. When you
plan a study block in Syllabus, the timer knows what it's for.

The other thing I'm proud of: it's local-first. No account required.
Your data lives in your browser, works offline, and you can export
it all. No "we might train on your notes" terms of service.

I'd love feedback — especially from students. What would make this
your daily driver?
```

## Launch-day playbook

- **T-0 (12:01 AM PST)**: Publish. Post the first comment within 5 minutes.
- **T+1h**: Reply to every comment. Ask follow-up questions.
- **T+3h**: Share on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, your personal network. Don't
  spam — one genuine post per platform.
- **All day**: Monitor comments, answer questions, fix nothing live
  (ship fixes after launch day).
- **End of day**: Thank everyone, note feedback for v2.

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Don't launch on Friday/weekend — engagement dies.
- Don't ask people to "upvote" explicitly — PH flags astroturfing.
  Ask them to check it out and leave honest feedback.
- Don't launch with a broken mobile experience — check it on your phone.
- Don't disappear after posting — PH rewards makers who engage.
