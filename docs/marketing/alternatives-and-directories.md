**Tags:** education, study, productivity, note-taking, flashcards,

**Short description** (250 chars):
```
Iterra is a free, local-first study workspace for students. Eight
tools — planner, flashcards, notes, timer, math workbench, attendance,
grading, and study materials — in one browser-based app. No account,
no subscription, works offline.
```

**Long description:**
```
Iterra replaces the eight apps students duct-tape together for studying:

• Boxed — time-blocking & brain-dump daily planner
• Syllabus — course planner & study schedule organizer
• Rhythm — habit & routine tracker with attendance logging
• Flashcards — spaced-repetition flashcard app with quizzes & drills
• Ink — linked note-taking app with wiki-links & daily pages
• Margin — study material organizer (recordings, PDFs, handouts)
• GradeStack — quiz builder & bubble-sheet grading tool
• Timer — pomodoro & focus timer with session ledger
• Proof — math problem workbench with error log

Unlike cloud-only study suites, Iterra is local-first: your data lives
in your browser, works offline, requires no account, and is exportable.
The tools share a live picture of your courses — your planner knows
when reviews are due, your notes surface in course dossiers, and
everything connects automatically.

Free forever. No subscription. No data harvesting.
```
