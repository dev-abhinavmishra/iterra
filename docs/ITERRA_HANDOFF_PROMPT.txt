PRODUCT GOAL

Iterra should be one calm, cohesive study workspace. It should give users substantially more value than the information and effort they put into it. A student should be able to enter their courses or paste a schedule once, then see organized deadlines, next actions, and a realistic study plan. Starting a timer should bring up an individualized, time-budgeted session that opens the exact relevant assignment, flashcard deck, calculation, or note. Real progress should flow back to the right place. The next session should build on it. A note, graded assessment, or task list should similarly turn into usable study work with minimal re-entry.

Make useful defaults and one clear next action do the heavy lifting. Keep deeper controls available when needed. Prioritize truthful data, predictable keyboard and mobile use, accessible design, fast cold loading and warm navigation, recovery from ordinary mistakes, and durability after reloads, retries, route changes, and offline use. The full goal covers the real workflows of the entire app, including empty, populated, error, offline, and recovery states. Keep the full scope open until its release gates are supported by evidence.
